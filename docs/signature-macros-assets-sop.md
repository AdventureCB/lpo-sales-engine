# Signatures, Macros & Assets SOP — Sales Rep Guide

*How to set up your email signature and build your outreach library — the macros and assets that make sending fast and consistent. Companion to the [Rep SOP](https://app.clickup.com/90141363968/docs/2kydg3r0-2594) and the [AI Features SOP](docs/ai-features-sop.md). Questions and corrections → Kyle.*

---

## The big picture

Three things save you from typing the same things over and over:

- **Signature** — your sign-off, added to every email automatically. Set once.
- **Macros** — reusable message templates (call them canned responses) for email, text, or WhatsApp. Insert one and it fills in the customer's name and deal details for you.
- **Assets** — shared links and files (a build guide, a financing page, a spec sheet) you can drop into any message, or attach to a macro so they come along automatically.

Together, macros + assets are your **outreach library**. Set it up once and every message you send is faster and on-brand — and the AI draft-writer studies your library to sound like the team.

---

## 1. Email signature

Your signature appends to the bottom of **every email you send from the app** — you never type it.

**Set it up:** **Settings → My Profile → ✍️ Email signature.** It's a rich-text editor, so you can add formatting, your name, title, phone, and links. Write it, press **Save signature**, done.

> You only do this once. After that, every email — whether you typed it, used a macro, or used an AI draft — gets your signature added automatically. Don't put your signature *inside* macros; it would double up.

---

## 2. The library: templates vs. your macros

Open **Settings → Macro Library.** You'll see tabs: **My macros**, **Templates**, and **Assets**.

Here's the model, and it's the important part:

- **Templates** are a **shared catalog** the whole team can add to. Think of them as the master list of good messages.
- **My macros** are **your personal copies.** When you toggle a template **on**, you get an editable copy in *My macros*. **Editing your copy never changes the shared template** — so you can tweak the wording to sound like you without affecting anyone else.
- The composer (when you're sending an email or text on a deal) only ever offers **your** macros — the ones you've toggled on or created.

So the flow is: **browse Templates → toggle the good ones on → they appear in My macros → tweak to taste → use them when sending.**

You can also create a brand-new macro from scratch (**+ New macro**) — it adds it to the shared catalog *and* enables it for you in one step.

## 3. Building a macro

Each macro has:

| Field | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Channel** | Which medium it's for: **Email**, **Text (SMS)**, **WhatsApp**, or **Any**. Email macros get a rich-text body and a subject; the others are plain text. |
| **Name** | What you'll recognize it by in the picker (e.g. *"Financing intro"*). |
| **Folder** *(optional)* | Group related macros — e.g. *Follow-ups*, *Objections*, *Post-call*. Keeps a big library tidy. |
| **Subject** *(email only)* | The email subject line. |
| **Body** | The message itself. |

### Placeholders — the magic part

Instead of typing "Hi [name]" and filling it in every time, drop a **placeholder** into the body. It fills itself in from the deal when you send. In the editor, use the **+ Insert placeholder** dropdown. Available tokens:

| Placeholder | Fills in |
|---|---|
| `{{first_name}}` | The customer's first name |
| `{{last_name}}` | Their last name |
| `{{name}}` | Their full name |
| `{{deal_title}}` | The deal's title |
| `{{truck}}` | Their truck model |
| `{{rep_name}}` | Your name |

Example body:
> Hi `{{first_name}}`, following up on your build for the `{{truck}}`. Happy to walk you through financing whenever you're ready — just reply here. — `{{rep_name}}`

When you send that on a deal, it becomes: *"Hi Eric, following up on your build for the Tacoma…"* automatically. Write the macro once; it personalizes every time.

## 4. Assets — shared links & files

The **Assets** tab holds things any rep can drop into a message. Two kinds:

- **🔗 URL assets** — a link (a build guide, a comparison page, a financing page). When used, it inserts as **clickable link text** in the message.
- **📎 Media assets** — an uploaded file (a spec sheet PDF, a photo). When used, it **attaches to the email.**

**To add one:**
- **URL asset**: paste a name and the `https://…` link, press **Add URL**.
- **Media asset**: give it a name, press **📎 Upload media**, pick the file.

Assets are **shared across the team** — build a good one once and everyone can use it.

### Attaching assets to a macro

When you build or edit a macro, you can **pre-assign assets** to it (under "Pre-assigned links & attachments"). Then whenever you use that macro, its assets come along automatically — URLs drop in as links, media attaches to the email. This is how you make a "send the financing packet" macro that pulls the financing page link *and* the rate sheet PDF in one click.

---

## 5. Using macros & assets when you send

On a deal page (or in the dialer), open the email or text composer in the comm bar. Pick one of **your macros** and it drops into the draft — **placeholders already filled in** with this customer's name and details, and any **pre-assigned assets** pulled in (links in the body, files attached). Edit anything you like, then send.

Your signature is added to emails automatically at send time — you'll see it arrive on the customer's end even though it's not in the draft box.

---

## 6. How this powers the AI

Your macros and assets aren't just for manual sends — the **AI draft writer studies them.** When you press "Draft email/text" in the 🗒 Scripts & drafts card, the AI reads the team's macros to match our **voice, tone, and length**, and it knows what each asset link contains so it only includes one when it genuinely helps. So a well-built library makes *both* your manual sends and your AI drafts better. (More in the [AI Features SOP](docs/ai-features-sop.md).)

---

## Setup checklist for a new rep

1. **Write your signature** (Settings → My Profile). One time.
2. **Open the Templates tab** and toggle on the macros you'll actually use.
3. **Tweak your copies** in My macros so they sound like you — add placeholders where you'd normally type a name.
4. **Skim the Assets tab** so you know what links and files exist to drop into messages.
5. **Organize with folders** once you have more than a handful of macros.

That's a 15-minute setup that pays off every single day.

---

*Edit your own copies freely — it never touches anyone else's. Assets and templates are shared, so if you build a great one, the whole team benefits.*
