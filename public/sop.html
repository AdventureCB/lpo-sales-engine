1First-day setup

  1. Log in with your @lonepeakoverland.com account. Stay logged in — the app keeps your session.
  2. Allow the microphone when prompted. Calls run through the app in your browser window — no separate phone app, no cell phone needed.
  3. Connect Gmail from the sidebar (Connect Gmail). This lets you send email from the app and pulls your customer email threads onto deal timelines automatically.
  4. Set your signature and ringtone under Settings → My Profile. Your signature is appended to every email you send from the app; the ringtone picker has a preview button.
  5. Click anywhere once after opening the app. Browsers block audio until your first click — if you never click, inbound calls ring silently.
Tip — second window You can open a second companion window to keep the CRM up while you dial (ask an admin to show you the aux-window link). The app prevents two windows from showing the same page, and your phone always lives in your main window — a second window will say "phone active in another window," which is normal.

2The daily rhythm

  1. Check the bell (top of the app): overnight voicemails, missed calls, inbound texts, @mentions, and new leads land there.
  2. Check the Hot List: deals showing buying signals right now. These come first — signals decay fast.
  3. Generate your sprint list and start dialing. Lists are built by the ranking ladder (press the ℹ️ button on the Sprint Lists page for exactly how deals are chosen and ranked).
  4. Work the dialer through the list. Reply to texts and emails between calls — response speed is the single strongest thing you control.
  5. Log everything as you go. If it isn't in the app, it didn't happen — follow-ups, notes, outcomes.
Why speed matters Our own closed-deal data shows deals first-called within 24 hours of arriving win at several times the base rate, and high-intent sources (Saved Builds, Abandoned Carts) close fastest. When a fresh lead appears, it outranks almost anything else you could do.

3Calling

From the dialer

  • The dialer walks your sprint list deal by deal. While you review a lead, the AI pre-builds a call outline (StoryBrand-based: their story, your plan, discovery questions, likely objections) in the 🗒 Scripts & drafts card.
  • Press dial, have the conversation, hang up — then pick a disposition: Connected, VM left, No answer, Bad number, or Callback. Add a note and a follow-up (1 week / 2 weeks / 1 month / custom date / none).
  • Dialer hotkeys are shown on the buttons; they automatically pause while you're typing in any text box.

From a deal page

Press the call button on any deal. A floating panel appears bottom-right with a timer, mute, and end-call — and when you hang up it walks you through the same disposition + follow-up flow as the dialer. Never skip the disposition; it drives cooldowns, stats, and list ranking.

Inbound calls

  • Calls to your number ring in the app on any page, with a banner to answer or ignore. Answering from behind another window pulls the app forward.
  • If you're already on a call, a second caller goes straight to your voicemail — your live call is never interrupted. The missed call + voicemail appear in your bell; call back when you wrap.
  • Voicemails show up with a transcript in the bell, on the deal timeline, and in the Call log — press a row there to play the audio and read the transcript.
Rule All calls and texts go through the app. Quo is gone for sales — anything sent from old apps or a personal phone doesn't reach customers reliably and is invisible to the team.

4Texting

  • Text from a deal page (comm bar) or the Texts page. Conversations open in a Messenger-style dock at the bottom of the screen.
  • Inbound texts auto-open their chat and show a banner at the top of the app, whatever page you're on.
  • Attach photos with the 🖼 button. Inbound photo/video messages show on the deal timeline (expand the entry).
  • Opt-outs are enforced. If a customer texted STOP, the app blocks your send and offers a compliant opt-in request instead. Don't fight it — it's the law, and the system logs everything.

5Email

  • Send from the deal page's comm bar — the email goes out from your Gmail with your signature and logs to the timeline instantly.
  • Reply to inbound email straight from the timeline: expand an inbound email (📥) and press ↩ Reply. Your reply threads properly into the customer's inbox conversation.
  • Open & click tracking: sent emails show "👁 Opened ×2 · 🔗 1 click" on the timeline once activity arrives. Treat clicks as truth and opens as a hint — some mail apps fake opens, others hide them.
  • Attach files from the shared asset library; links you include are tracked automatically.

6Working a deal

The deal page is the cockpit

  • Timeline: every call (with transcript once processed), text, email, note, and system event. Click entries to expand; call entries with real transcripts offer ⚖ Review call (see §8).
  • Upcoming: your scheduled follow-ups. Completing them logs to the timeline.
  • Marketing signals & ad journey: what the buyer has been opening, clicking, and which ads brought them in — read this before you dial.
  • Saved build links: click a link in the timeline to copy the complete URL — always share the full link, never retype it.

Losing a deal — use the categories

When a deal is dead, press Lost and pick the category. This matters — each one triggers different automation:

CategoryWhat happens
DNCCustomer asked us to stop. Marked lost immediately and the contact is protected — the system will never call, text, or list them again. Absolute.
No interestDeal is unassigned and returns to the re-prospecting pool for a future pass.
No contact madeSame — unassigned, back to the pool.
Competitor purchaseRequires the competitor's name, then marks lost. That name feeds our positioning.
Not qualifiedUnassigned, back to the pool.
DuplicateMarked lost automatically. If the other deal is the keeper, use ⧉ Merge instead — merge moves the whole history onto the surviving deal and deletes this one.
Rule DNC is sacred. If a customer says any version of "stop contacting me," category 1 — immediately, no exceptions, no one re-adds them.

7Sprint lists & hot list

  • Sprint lists are generated fresh and ranked by the ladder (deal quality, signals, timezone fit). Checking out a list reserves those deals for you for up to 3 days — finish what you check out.
  • New reps: if you don't own enough qualifying deals yet, your lists auto-fill from the unassigned pool with the same ranking rules — working them is how you build your book.
  • Deals you've dialed recently sit in cooldown and won't reappear immediately; snoozed deals return when their snooze ends.
  • The Hot List flags deals showing live buying signals (email opens, clicks, multiple signal types in a window). A hot flag means call today — a task is created for you automatically.

8The AI assistant

The buyer profile

  • Every worked deal builds a profile below Marketing Signals: who this buyer is (archetype fit), what's known (attributes with confidence), specifics worth remembering (tags), and a suggested next action with discovery questions.
  • Correct it — that's the point. Press ✕ on anything wrong (wrong archetype, bad tag) and it stays gone. Press ✎ Tell the AI to state a fact you know ("wife's name is Sara, budget is firm at 9k") — the profile rebuilds instantly treating your note as truth. Your corrections outrank the AI, always.

Scripts & drafts

  • The 🗒 Scripts & drafts card writes call outlines, emails, and texts voiced like our team, tailored to this buyer. Pick a theme (⭐ marks the suggested one) or type a direction for something specific.
  • One click inserts a draft into the composer — edit before sending; you own every word that goes out.
  • Press 👎 with a short note when a draft misses. The system literally learns your standards from this.

Call reviews

After a real conversation, open the call on the timeline and press ⚖ Review call. You get a StoryBrand scorecard (guide positioning, problem articulation, simple plan, clear CTA, discovery), what worked, and one thing to do differently. Review your good and bad calls both — a few reviews in, the AI starts tailoring your call outlines to your specific coaching focus.

9House rules

  • Everything in the app. Calls, texts, email, notes. No personal phones, no outside tools — customers fall through cracks the moment work happens off-system.
  • DNC is absolute (see §6).
  • Disposition every call — cooldowns, lists, and your stats depend on it.
  • Log the follow-up before you move on. Future-you will not remember.
  • Speed to lead beats everything. Fresh leads and hot flags first, then the list.
  • Unassigned ≠ unloved. Pool deals are fair game via your sprint lists; working them grows your book.

"This page is open in another window"Also normal — two windows can't edit the same page. Use it in the other window, or take over from the prompt.
Inbound calls don't ring audiblyClick anywhere in the app once (browsers mute audio until first interaction). Check your ringtone in My Profile.

Black screen / "Application error"The app auto-reloads once on its own. If it doesn't recover, quit and reopen — the crash is already reported automatically.
Email won't sendReconnect Gmail from the sidebar — tokens occasionally expire.
Anything elseTell Kyle with the page you were on and the time — crash telemetry usually has the details already.
